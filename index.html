Shri Ganesh की कथा

Hindu parampara mein Ganesh ji ko aarambh, buddhi aur mangal ke devta ke roop mein shraddha se smaran kiya jaata hai. Kisi shubh kaarya se pehle unka smaran karne ki parampara bahut vyaapak hai.

Pauranik kathaaon mein Mata Parvati dwaara Ganesh ji ke praakatya aur unke Shiv ji se punarmilan ki katha vishesh roop se prasiddh hai. Yeh katha mata-pita ke prati prem, kartavya aur samarpan ka bhaav prastut karti hai.

Radha-Krishna ki Katha

Shri Krishna ki baal aur kishor leelaon ka sambandh Brajbhoomi se joda jaata hai. Radha-Krishna ki bhakti parampara mein unka sambandh divya prem, samarpan aur aatmik bhakti ka prateek maana jaata hai.

Bhakti sahitya mein Radha ko Krishna ke prati poorn prem aur samarpan ki aadarsh bhakt ke roop mein dekha jaata hai. Vrindavan ki paramparaon mein Radha-Krishna ki leelaon ka smaran prem ko aadhyaatmik saadhna se jodta hai.

Shiv-Parvati ki Katha

Mata Parvati ko Himavan ki putri aur Shiv ji ki sahadharmini ke roop mein pauranik parampara mein varnit kiya jaata hai. Parvati ji ki tapasya aur Shiv ji se vivaah ki katha anek granthon aur lokparamparaon mein milti hai.

Shiv aur Shakti ki upaasana mein dono ko chetna aur shakti ke paraspar poorak roopon ke roop mein samjha jaata hai. Isi kaaran unka yugl swaroop santulan, parivaar aur aadhyaatmik ekta ka sundar prateek maana jaata hai.

Shri Hanuman की कथा

Ramayan parampara mein Hanuman ji ko Shri Ram ke mahaan bhakt, doot aur sahayogi ke roop mein smaran kiya jaata hai. Unki bhakti, saahas, vinamrata aur seva-bhaav Bharatiya bhakti parampara mein vishesh sthaan rakhta hai.

Lanka ki yaatra, Sita ji ka sandesh Shri Ram tak pahunchaana aur Ram-Ravan yuddh mein sahayog karna Hanuman ji se judi pramukh Ramayan kathaaon mein shaamil hain.
